### Government Policy & Data

* [National Waste Policy (NEPC)](https://www.nepc.gov.au/projects/national-waste-policy) - Australia’s core national framework for waste management and resource recovery, outlining long-term policy directions and strategies.
* [National Waste and Resource Recovery Report 2024](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/national-waste-and-resource-recovery-report-2024.pdf) - Provides comprehensive national data, trends, and performance metrics for Australia’s waste and recycling sector.
* [Victoria's Implementation Plan for the National Waste Policy](https://www.vic.gov.au/victorias-implementation-plan-national-waste-policy) - Details how Victoria is implementing national waste targets and circular economy initiatives.
* [Recycling Victoria](https://www.vic.gov.au/about-recycling-victoria) - Explains Victoria’s waste and recycling reforms, regulatory framework, and circular economy objectives.
* [Our Role: Recycling Victoria](https://www.vic.gov.au/our-role-recycling-victoria) - Clarifies the responsibilities of key government agencies involved in waste management and resource recovery across Victoria.

### Industry, Planning & Practical Resources

* [Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation (APCO)](https://apco.org.au/) - Leads national packaging sustainability initiatives and provides tools, guidance, and reporting resources for businesses.
* [Planet Ark Recycling Near You](https://recyclingnearyou.com.au/) - Helps households and businesses find local recycling options for a wide range of materials across Australia.
* [Statewide Waste and Resource Recovery Infrastructure Plan (SWRRIP)](https://www.sustainability.vic.gov.au/about-us/state-government-strategic-plans/statewide-waste-and-resource-recovery-infrastructure-plan-swrrip) - Offers strategic guidance on waste infrastructure planning, resource recovery technologies, and investment opportunities.
* [Victorian Waste Education Strategy](https://www.sustainability.vic.gov.au/about-us/state-government-strategic-plans/victorian-waste-education-strategy) - Provides educational frameworks and behaviour-change resources to improve waste reduction and recycling outcomes.
* [Waste Management and Resource Recovery Association of Australia (WMRR)](https://www.wmrr.asn.au/) - Australia’s leading industry association offering policy updates, technical resources, training, and sector advocacy.
